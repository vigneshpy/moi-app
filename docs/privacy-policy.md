# Privacy Policy — Moi App

**Last updated:** April 14, 2026

## Overview

Moi ("the App") is a local-only gift tracking application. The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

## Data Storage

All data you enter into the App (event names, guest names, gift amounts, notes, and cover photos) is stored exclusively on your device using SQLite. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

## Data Collection

The App does **not** collect:
- Personal information
- Location data
- Analytics or usage data
- Advertising identifiers
- Crash reports

## Third-Party Services

The App does **not** use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect user data.

## Data Sharing

The App does **not** share any data with third parties. The only way data leaves your device is if you explicitly use the Export feature (CSV/PDF), which creates a file on your device that you choose to share via your phone's share sheet.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time via **Settings → Clear all data** within the App. Uninstalling the App also removes all stored data.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
